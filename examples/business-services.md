## Before you post

- Confirm: your real plan items, exclusions and any uptime or response-time promises are in writing (ideas 1, 2, 13, 14)
- Confirm: no real client screens, documents, data, names or logos appear in any frame; get the client's OK for idea 4 (ideas 3, 4, 11)
- Confirm: team members agree to appear and names and roles are right (ideas 7, 12)
- Confirm: have counsel or a HIPAA advisor read idea 9 before it goes out; law-office ethics rules on vendors are not covered here
- Confirm: backup, offsite and account-removal practices described are what you really do (ideas 8, 10, 11)
- Confirm: all music, photos and clips are yours or licensed (idea 4 and any background track)
- Rule: Never call a setup unhackable or breach-proof; show what a safeguard does ([15 U.S. Code 45, FTC Act section 5](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/45), read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: Say so plainly when a post is paid or gifted ([FTC Endorsement Guides Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking), read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: Disclose vendor or brand ties when you feature one ([16 CFR 255.5](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5), read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: Never invent a review, reviewer or customer words ([16 CFR 465.2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/465.2), read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: Use only photos, clips and music you have the right to use ([Copyright Basics, Circular 1](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ01.pdf), read 2026-11-20)
