## Before you post

- Confirm: salon name, stylist names and specialties (idea 5)
- Confirm: wait time before washing, and that you give this advice (ideas 7, 13)
- Confirm: weekday times, openings and any offer; never publish a price or discount that is not real (ideas 10, 12, 15)
- Confirm: tool-cleaning steps match your routine and Texas licensing rules (idea 11)
- Confirm: every client on camera agreed to be filmed (ideas 4, 9, 15)
- Confirm: you patch test before every color service, as the videos say (ideas 1, 4, 6)
- Rule: a hair color short never skips the patch test. Source: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/hair-dyes
- Rule: never say a cosmetic or salon service grows hair or treats a condition. Source: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/it-cosmetic-drug-or-both-or-it-soap
- Rule: say plainly in the post if a brand paid or gifted you or has a tie to you. Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
- Rule: disclose material connections to vendors or brands you feature. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5
- Rule: never invent a review, reviewer or customer quote. Source: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/465.2
- Rule: use only photos, clips and music you have the right to use. Source: https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ01.pdf
