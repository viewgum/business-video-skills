## Before you post

- Confirm: the county and the van details, and that anyone on camera agreed (ideas 1, 4, 7, 15).
- Confirm: each client's dog and owner agreed to be filmed, and no client name, address or story is shown (ideas 2, 3, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12).
- Confirm: open dates, rebooking routine and any booking link before posting ideas 10, 12 and 14 [owner to add].
- Confirm: keep ideas 5, 9 and 13 general and point to a vet for a particular dog.
- Rule: Explain in general; never diagnose a viewer's pet or prescribe or dose a treatment ([21 CFR 530.3](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/530.3))
- Rule: Say so in the post if anyone is paid or gifted for a post about the business ([FTC Endorsement Guides Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking))
- Rule: Disclose a vendor or brand tie, such as a shampoo brand that gives you product ([16 CFR 255.5](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5))
- Rule: Never invent or alter a review, reviewer or customer's words ([16 CFR 465.2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/465.2))
- Rule: Use only photos, clips and music you own or have permission for ([Copyright Basics](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ01.pdf))
