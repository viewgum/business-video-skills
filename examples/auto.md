## Before you post

- Confirm: shop name, town, and which services you offer: brakes, tires, alignment, battery tests, brake fluid [all guessed]
- Confirm: nothing on screen is a made-up price, discount, hour, award, years in business or customer quote; none is in this plan (ideas 12 and 10: [owner to add] or leave out)
- Confirm: customer permission and no plates, VINs or faces in frame (ideas 3, 7, 12)
- Confirm: no promise a car is safe after a service; say what was checked or replaced (ideas 14, 15)
- Confirm: if a brand is shown or recommended and it pays you, gives you something or is tied to you, say so in the post (idea 13)
- Rule: When someone is paid or given something for a post about your business, say so in the post. [FTC's Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking)
- Rule: Disclose a material connection to a vendor or brand you feature. [16 CFR 255.5](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5)
- Rule: No invented reviews, reviewers or customer words. [16 CFR 465.2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/465.2)
- Rule: Use only photos, clips and music you have the right to use, including trending audio. [Circular 1, Copyright Basics](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ01.pdf)
- Rule: State the general rule on warranties and third-party service, and point to the warranty itself (idea 8). [FTC says companies' warranty restrictions were illegal](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2022/07/ftc-says-companies-warranty-restrictions-were-illegal)
