## Before you post

- Confirm: the town, the team's real steps and timelines, and any figures marked [owner to add] (ideas 3, 8, 10, 12, 15)
- Confirm: the seller's permission to film any listing, and that no tenants, buyers or neighbors appear (ideas 2, 9, 11)
- Confirm: the NC disclosure form wording is the current form (idea 6)
- Confirm: photos, clips and music are yours or licensed
- Rule: describe the home and the place, never who it suits (Fair Housing Act advertising rule): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/24/100.75
- Rule: present a true picture, no promised price or time (REALTOR Code of Ethics, Article 12): https://www.nar.realtor/about-nar/governing-documents/code-of-ethics/2026-code-of-ethics-standards-of-practice
- Rule: disclose paid or gifted posts: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
- Rule: disclose material connections to vendors: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5
- Rule: no fake or altered reviews: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/465.2
- Rule: use only media you have rights to: https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ01.pdf
