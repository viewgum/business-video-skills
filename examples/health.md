## Claim check

Ran the checker on all 15 titles, hooks, beats and captions: nothing flagged. Read by hand as well:
- No cure, relief, result or prevention promises. Ideas 6 and 14 describe what a thing is without saying it stops cavities.
- No patients appear in any idea, so no HIPAA authorization is needed. Idea 12 uses a model or stock image, not a real patient's X-ray.
- No reviews, testimonials, prices, discounts or awards are used. Idea 13's years in practice is a placeholder for the owner.
- The checker cannot see pictures, so watch each video for patient names, charts or screens in the background.

## Before you post

- Confirm: staff consent in writing for each person on camera (ideas 1, 3, 10, 12, 13)
- Confirm: no patient, chart, schedule or screen visible in any shot (ideas 1, 3, 8, 12)
- Confirm: dentist reviews the wording of every explainer (ideas 4, 6, 7, 9, 11, 14)
- Confirm: your practice's name, suburb, dentist names, years in practice, and how patients book [owner to add] (ideas 13, 15)
- Confirm: sealants are offered at your practice (idea 14)
- Confirm: music, images and tooth models are yours or licensed (all ideas)
- Rule: Explain how the body works in general; promise no cure, relief or result. [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance)
- Rule: Keep real patients, and anything identifying them, out unless they signed an authorization. [45 CFR 164.508](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/45/164.508)
- Rule: Say so when a post is paid or gifted, or a vendor has a tie to you. [FTC Endorsement Guides](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) and [16 CFR 255.5](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5)
- Rule: Never invent a review or a customer's words. [16 CFR 465.2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/465.2)
- Rule: Use only photos, clips and music you have the right to use. [Copyright Basics](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ01.pdf)
