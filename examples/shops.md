- Confirm: you really use the angled cut, and film the real fix and the real side-by-side (ideas 3, 5, 15)
- Confirm: no customer names, addresses or faces in frame without consent (ideas 6, 7, 12)
- Confirm: the shop can make lily-free bouquets (idea 9)

- Rule: Never call an arrangement with lilies safe for a home with cats. The FDA says lilies are very dangerous for cats, including the vase water. https://www.fda.gov/animal-veterinary/animal-health-literacy/lovely-lilies-and-curious-cats-dangerous-combination (read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: Say plainly in the post if someone was paid or given something to feature your business or a vendor. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking (read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: Disclose a business, family or personal tie to a brand or vendor you feature. https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5 (read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: Never invent a review, reviewer or customer quote. https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/465.2 (read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: Use only photos, clips and music you have the right to use. https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ01.pdf (read 2026-11-20)
