## Before you post

- Confirm: preorder method, cutoff, pickup hours and prices before posts 2, 5, 9 and 12 go out (ideas 2, 5, 9, 12)
- Confirm: roaster name and place, and any tie to the roaster, which must be said in the post (idea 7)
- Confirm: every bagel history fact has a source (idea 15)
- Confirm: staff are fine being filmed, and no customer faces without permission (ideas 3, 9)
- Confirm: do the flour demo for real and show the real weights (idea 11)
- Do not call anything allergen-free, nut-free or gluten-free unless you checked ingredients, suppliers and cross-contact. The FDA lists nine major allergens: milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans and sesame.
- Rule: allergen-free claims ([Food Allergies, FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/food-allergies), read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: gluten-free definition, 20 parts per million ([FDA gluten-free labeling Q&A](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/questions-and-answers-gluten-free-food-labeling-final-rule), read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: paid or gifted posts say so in the post ([FTC Endorsement Guides Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking), read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: disclose business, family or personal ties to a vendor ([16 CFR 255.5](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5), read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: never invent a review or a customer's words ([16 CFR 465.2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/465.2), read 2026-11-20)
- Rule: use only photos, clips and music you have the right to use ([Copyright Basics, Circular 1](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ01.pdf), read 2026-11-20)
